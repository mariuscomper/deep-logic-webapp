    How it was scored, and how far off it may be

    Delta is a score from 0 to 100 for how much good an object does at its best landing. It combines four scores from 0 to 10, which I wrote: Need (30%), Copy (25%), Trust (20%) and Ripple (25%). I chose the weights too.

    Dates and figures come from the sources linked under each object. The scores are judgements, and the page shows how they held up.
