793 – 1066 • Scandinavia, Europe, the North Atlantic, and the road east

Viking Journeys Across the Known World

Everyone knows about the raid on Lindisfarne. Fewer know the same restless energy carried Norse ships to Baghdad's markets, Constantinople's walls, Newfoundland's shores, and a Duchy of their own in France. This map traces where they actually went.
